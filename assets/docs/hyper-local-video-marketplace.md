### Phase I Strategy: Hyper-Local Video Marketplace ("2027 Craigslist")

This proposal merges short-form video engagement with hyper-local trade dynamics, aiming directly at the core demographic of young adults aged 21 to 35.

---

### Key Product Pillars

#### 1. Foundational Social Engine

* **User Accounts & Profiles:** Verification via phone number and local neighborhood pin.
* **Core Interactions:** Like, comment, share, bookmark, and follow local sellers or creators.
* **In-App Messaging (DM):** Direct buyer-to-seller chat thread linked directly to specific listed items.

#### 2. Short-Form Video Creation & Feed Mechanics

* **Immersive Full-Screen Video Feed:** Vertical video discovery tailored to show item demos, unboxings, or local "garage sale" tours.
* **Interactive Purchase Overlays:** Viewers tap directly on an item floating in the video to view its price, condition, and distance.

---

### Geo-Location Engine & Local Radius Constraints

To create a neighborhood-first trading ecosystem, physical proximity acts as the primary content filter:

---

* **Strict 100-Mile Radius Limit:** Users only discover listings within a maximum 100-mile geographical boundary.
* **Neighborhood Proximity Pins:** Shows coarse distance (e.g., "3.2 miles away in Downtown") without exposing precise home address coordinates.
* **In-Person Meetup Scheduling:** Integrated chat module to set verified safe-exchange locations (police station parking lots, public plazas).

---

### AI Value & Negotiation Agents

#### Seller Assistant: Automated Valuation & Listing Optimization

* **Computer Vision Valuation:** The seller records a 5-second video of an item; the AI analyzes make, model, wear condition, and active local comps to recommend an optimal listing price.
* **Auto-Generated Captions:** AI generates dynamic titles, tags, and condition descriptions directly from video frames.

#### Buyer Assistant: "The Real Talk" Deal Consultant

* **Price Fairness Analyzer:** Evaluates current market price versus seller listing price.
* **Sassy Consumer Persona:** Delivers unfiltered feedback on bad deals.
