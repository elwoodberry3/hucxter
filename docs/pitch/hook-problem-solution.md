## Slide 1 — The Hook (The Vision)

### The best deals are down the street.

**The Shift**
Commerce went global and forgot that the best transactions happen right down the street.

**The Vision**
Hucxter is a hyperlocal marketplace built on short-form video. Film an item in seconds, and
neighbors within driving distance find it, message you, and pick it up in person. No shipping.

**The Status**
We're in closed alpha with our buyer/seller test team, proving the core loop: post, discover,
message, meet. Next: an invite-only launch in [launch area]. Goal: 10,000 local users in
[timeframe].

## Slide 2 — The Problem (The Market Failure)

### Local commerce is broken. Spam and pay-to-be-seen took it over.

**The Craigslist Decay**
The original home of local trade still works, but it hasn't kept up: text-heavy listings, a dated
experience, and constant spam.

**The Marketplace Trap**
Today's big social marketplaces bury local sellers behind paid boosts, opaque algorithms, and fake
accounts. Sellers pay to be seen. Buyers wade through noise.

**The Friction Point**
Buyers and sellers are stuck choosing between platforms that push shipping and fees and local
boards where it's hard to tell who's real. Static photos and text don't show what an item is
really like, or who's selling it.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution (The Product)

### Hucxter: Zero Shipping. Real Neighbors. Video-First.

**Show It, Don't List It**
A 10-second video replaces a static photo grid. Buyers see the item working, from every angle,
before they ever message. Every listing is local by design. You only see what you can pick up.

**Built for Trust**
Phone-verified accounts, layered bot and spam defenses, item-linked chat, one-tap reporting and
blocking, and suggested public meetup spots. The platform is designed from day one to keep
exchanges local, safe, and between real people.

**AI That Does the Busywork**
Record your item and Hucxter's AI drafts the title, category, and description for you to check
and post, so listing takes seconds instead of minutes. On our roadmap: AI pricing guidance based
on real local sales, and buyer-side deal checks.

**Purely Local**
A clean chat experience built for in-person pickup. No shipping. No hidden fees.

**What's Next**
Local creators and resellers who build a following around their finds, then honest, relevant
local advertising that helps buyers instead of interrupting them.
